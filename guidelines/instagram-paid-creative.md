# Instagram Paid Creative Guidelines

These guidelines give creative partners the brand, content, and design rules for producing Vera ads on Instagram. The campaign team supplies the objective, offer, landing experience, audience targeting, concepts, copy deliverables, and asset quantities.

## Brand Foundation

Vera helps people understand aesthetic treatments, receive personalized treatment recommendations, and find vetted providers when they are ready for a consultation. Clinical K-beauty shapes Vera's point of view: personalized care, thoughtful planning, ongoing maintenance, and a long-term relationship with skin.

Vera should feel editorial, clinically credible, culturally fluent, human, and restrained. Create desire through judgment and specificity rather than urgency or exaggerated promises.

## Audience

Speak to people navigating aesthetic treatment decisions who want personalized guidance, credible information, transparent pricing, and providers they can trust. Campaign-specific targeting comes from the campaign team.

"MAWFIAs" is internal audience shorthand only. Never use it in ads, campaign copy, presentations, or other external communications.

## Voice and Copy

- Use calm authority and natural, conversational language.
- Build each ad around one clear idea.
- Lead with the audience's question, situation, or benefit.
- Explain clinical context when it improves understanding.
- Translate Korean terms the first time they appear.
- Avoid fear, shame, pressure, anti-aging language, generic beauty promises, and unsupported superlatives.
- Never use emoji in creative.
- Do not place internal test names, audience labels, or version numbers on published creative.
- Use the offer, call to action, and campaign copy approved for the active campaign.

## Claims and Product Boundaries

- Vera's facial analysis provides personalized treatment recommendations. It is not a medical diagnosis.
- The analysis does not screen for contraindications or determine clinical appropriateness.
- A qualified provider determines clinical appropriateness and the final treatment plan.
- Do not promise guaranteed results or imply that one treatment is appropriate for everyone.
- Medical, safety, efficacy, treatment, provider, and credential claims must come from approved source material.
- Do not invent Vera Verified criteria, provider credentials, prices, testimonials, or outcomes.

## Logo

Use the [approved logo files](../assets/logos/README.md).

- Use the full wordmark by default.
- Use the V mark only when space cannot support the full wordmark.
- Use coral or dark on light backgrounds and white on dark backgrounds or photography.
- Preserve the mark exactly. Do not type, redraw, recolor, crop, or apply effects.

## Color

- Vera Coral: `#B5373A`
- Dark mark: `#2D2926`
- Ink: `#1C1917`
- Warm White: `#FAFAF9`
- Muted Stone: `#78716C`
- Light Border: `#E7E5E4`

Warm neutrals, white space, and photography should carry most of the composition. Keep coral to 10% or less of the surface and use it to direct attention.

See the complete [Color System](../foundations/colors.md).

## Typography

- Use Cormorant Garamond selectively for display headlines and editorial moments.
- Use Geist for supporting copy, labels, captions, and information.
- Use Vera Custom only for the logo wordmark.
- Prioritize mobile legibility. Shorten copy instead of shrinking it to fit.

See the complete [Typography System](../foundations/typography.md).

## Layout and Imagery

- Use generous margins, clear hierarchy, and one focal point.
- Use real skin, providers, clinics, treatment moments, and product screens when approved.
- Favor natural light, visible skin texture, and editorial crops.
- Keep text away from eyes, mouths, and other important facial features.
- Avoid generic medspa stock, over-retouching, crowded infographics, decorative clinical icons, and excessive gradients.
- Do not use before-and-after imagery in Vera Instagram ads.

See [Visual Direction](../visual-direction.md) and [Do's and Don'ts](./dos-and-donts.md).

## Instagram Specifications

### Feed

- Canvas: `1080 × 1350` pixels (`4:5`)
- Keep critical content at least `100` pixels above the bottom edge.

### Stories and Reels

- Canvas: `1080 × 1920` pixels (`9:16`)
- Keep critical text at least `250` pixels below the top.
- Keep critical text and the logo at least `300` pixels above the bottom.
- Keep the focal point and essential information within the middle 70% of the frame.

### Creative Rules

- Design for phone-size legibility and strong contrast.
- Establish one hierarchy: focal image or product moment, headline, then logo.
- Do not bake a CTA button into the image. Meta supplies the CTA interface.
- Caption all video and place essential spoken information on screen.
- Make the first three seconds of motion understandable without sound.
- Keep test labels and version names in filenames, never in the creative.
- Ensure carousel cards can stand alone because Instagram may deliver a later card first.
