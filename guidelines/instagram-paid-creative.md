# Instagram Paid Creative Guidelines

Use these guidelines with Vera's existing [Brand Identity](../brand-identity.md), [Visual Direction](../visual-direction.md), [Typography](../foundations/typography.md), and [Color System](../foundations/colors.md).

The campaign partner determines the campaign objective, offer, audience targeting, landing experience, concepts, copy, and required asset quantities.

## Creative Direction

Paid social can be more expressive than Vera's app and website. It should remain recognizably Vera, but it does not need to reproduce the product design system.

Typography-driven creative and K-beauty-led messaging have performed well for Vera in past paid campaigns. Use them as creative starting points, not mandatory formulas.

- Build each ad around one clear idea.
- Use strong, editorial typography that remains legible on a phone.
- Photography is optional. Typography can carry the entire ad.
- K-beauty messaging should communicate a specific cultural insight, practice, or point of view.
- Avoid stereotypes, broad cultural generalizations, or treating Korean popularity as proof of effectiveness.
- Korean language used as a visual element must be accurate.

## Typography

Core Vera typography:

- **Geist:** Supporting copy, labels, and informational text.
- **Cormorant Garamond:** Display headlines and editorial moments.
- **Vera Custom:** Logo use only.

Approved paid-social extension:

- **DM Serif Display Regular and Italic:** Large headlines and expressive italic emphasis. Available from [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Serif+Display).

Paid creative may use oversized typography, expressive italics, tighter crops, and stronger scale changes. Avoid novelty fonts or unrelated visual styles.

## Color

Both Vera coral values are approved for paid social:

- Vera Coral: `#B5373A`
- Bright Coral: `#D85959`

Paid creative may also use:

- Deep Maroon: `#5C1A1A`
- Terracotta: `#C85A3E`
- Warm Ink: `#3E1212`
- Warm White: `#FAFAF9`
- White and warm dark neutrals

Tonal gradients and deeper warm backgrounds are permitted. Keep the overall palette warm and recognizably Vera. Avoid neon colors and cool-dominant palettes.

## Voice and Copy

- Use calm authority and natural, conversational language.
- Lead with a clear audience insight, question, tension, or benefit.
- Keep each ad focused on one message.
- Translate Korean terms the first time they appear.
- Avoid fear, shame, pressure, unsupported superlatives, and generic beauty promises.
- Do not use internal audience terms such as "MAWFIAs" externally.
- The offer and call to action must match the active campaign.

## Claims

- Do not present an assessment, recommendation, or consultation as a medical diagnosis.
- Do not promise guaranteed results or imply that one treatment is appropriate for everyone.
- Treatment, safety, efficacy, provider, and credential claims must come from approved source material.
- Do not invent provider criteria, credentials, prices, testimonials, or outcomes.
- Campaign copy must accurately reflect what the associated landing experience provides.
- Do not use before-and-after imagery unless the campaign explicitly approves it and its source, consent, and claims have been verified.

## Meta Content Restrictions

- Do not state or imply personal attributes about the viewer.
- Do not use shame, fear, or negative self-perception.
- Do not promise guaranteed or unrealistic results.
- Do not present recommendations as medical diagnoses.
- Treatment and provider claims must use approved information.
- The ad's offer, claims, and call to action must match the landing experience.

## Logo

Use the [approved logo files](../assets/logos/README.md).

- Use the full wordmark by default.
- Use the V mark when space cannot support the wordmark.
- Use coral or dark on light backgrounds and white on dark backgrounds.
- Preserve the original artwork and proportions.

## Photography and Image Sources

Vera uses photography sourced from Unsplash and AI-generated imagery.

- Favor natural light, visible skin texture, warm tones, and editorial composition.
- When featuring women, represent a broad range of skin tones and racial and ethnic backgrounds. Do not default to showing only white or East Asian women.
- Avoid generic medspa stock imagery and heavily retouched or artificial-looking skin.
- Do not use AI imagery to fabricate treatment results, testimonials, patients, or providers.
- Do not imply that an AI-generated person is a real Vera customer or clinician.
- Confirm usage rights and retain the source link for third-party photography.

## Layout

- Use one clear focal point and hierarchy.
- Maintain strong contrast and phone-size legibility.
- Use generous margins, but paid creative may employ tighter and more dramatic compositions than product design.
- Recompose each format instead of mechanically cropping one master.
- Keep important text away from faces and platform interface elements.
- Do not place internal test names or version labels on published creative.

## Instagram Formats

| Placement         | Size          |
| ----------------- | ------------- |
| Square            | `1080 × 1080` |
| Feed              | `1080 × 1350` |
| Stories and Reels | `1080 × 1920` |

- Keep critical content at least 150 pixels above the bottom of square creative.
- Keep critical content at least 100 pixels above the bottom of Feed creative.
- For Stories and Reels, keep important text at least 250 pixels below the top and important text and logos at least 300 pixels above the bottom.
- Keep essential Story and Reel information within the middle 70% of the frame.
- Do not bake a call-to-action button into the image. Meta supplies the interface.
- Caption video and make the opening understandable without sound.
- Ensure carousel cards can stand alone because Instagram may deliver a later card first.
